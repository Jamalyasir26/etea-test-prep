## §1 Style guide + glossary decisions

U1 glossary decisions applied and suite-pinned: تدریسیات (پیڈاگوجی banned), وضاحت, آپ address, Urdu punctuation (۔ ، ؟ ؛), verb-final restructuring, no calque, echo-gloss rule (restatement glosses dropped, meaning glosses kept). Maths-specific decisions (brief §3 + coordinator adjudications; all flagged for owner confirmation in §8):

- **Numbers, equations, expressions and units are the TESTED MATERIAL — the defining rule for this bank:** every digit-group in the English source appears unchanged in the `_ur` rewrite (STRICT digit-group multiset equality on every field, suite-pinned); equations/expressions stay byte-identical (e.g. `360 / 8 = 45`); the multiplication `x` stays `x`; never "correct" a number, never round, never spell a source digit out as a word. 2914/3000 fields carry digits; baseline measured 0 digit-multiset mismatches.
- **Latin allowlist (suite-pinned):** single letters (variables/labels) + exactly 27 multi-letter words: SI units (cm, km, kg), function names (tan, sin, cos, sec, cosec, cot, log, pi — always Latin even in prose, `tan کی قیمت` never ٹین کی قیمت), acronyms (LCM, HCF, SAS, SSS, ASA, SSA, RHS), triangle labels (ABC, DEF, PQR, XYZ, LMN, STU), variable products (kx, mx), and Rs. `a.m.`/`p.m.` split into single letters by the tokenizer. No other Latin word may appear in any `_ur` field. `rhs` (5×, congruence) and `mx` (6×, `y = mx + c`) were added to the allowlist by coordinator adjudication after the full Latin inventory re-scan (102 distinct tokens).
- **Spelled-out English units → Urdu (brief §3.2, incl. options):** degrees→ڈگری (285×), years→سال, hours→گھنٹے / hour→گھنٹہ (oblique گھنٹے after میں/سے, فی گھنٹہ for per-hour, singular nominative گھنٹہ only in MAT-458), minutes→منٹ, seconds→سیکنڈ (gap), days→دن, litres→لیٹر, sq→مربع (`81 sq cm`→`81 مربع cm`, 67×; `مربع m` 9×), cubic→مکعب (15×), `square root of N`→`N کا جذر المربع`, مربع جذر→جذر المربع (12×, guide §2G; جذر المربع 41×, جذر المکعب 2× kept distinct), `P% of N`→`N کا P فیصد`, coefficient→ضریب/ضریبوں, x-intercept→x-مقطع / y-intercept→y-مقطع (gap), x-coordinate→x-مختصہ, x-axis→x-محور, like terms→ہم جنس جملے (gap), constant term→ثابت جملہ (replacing مستقل رقم, MAT-354), term (algebra)→جملہ, cylinder→استوانہ (gap), trapezium→ذوزنقہ (gap), head→چت / tail→پٹ incl. compounds چت-چت/چت-پٹ/پٹ-چت/پٹ-پٹ (gap, visible), ace→اکا (gap), `24-hour clock`→`24 گھنٹے والی گھڑی`, `1545/0705 hours`→bare readings `1545`/`0705` (flag), `painter hours`→رنگ ساز گھنٹے (gap), ٹین/سائن/کوسائن→tan/sin/cos, رداس→نصف قطر (dominant 16× form; نصف قطر 18×).
- **Formula word-labels translate:** length→لمبائی, width→چوڑائی, height→اونچائی, distance→فاصلہ, side→ضلع (but طرف kept where the bank uses it for triangle sides — see §7/§8: ضلع gender is inconsistent in the bank, MAT-273 "ہر ضلع" vs MAT-272 "ضلعیں").
- **Splits KEPT (not normalized — both forms fluent, distinct contexts):** تناسب (proportion, MAT-130 only — EN says "proportion", correct) vs نسبت (ratio, 15×) — match the EN word; ارتفاع ONLY in زاویہ ارتفاع/زاویہ انخفاض (7×/4×) vs اونچائی (height, 19×); احاطہ (perimeter, 14×) vs محیط (circumference, 8×); مفرد (prime/singular, 7×) vs مرکب (composite, 2×); عاد (number factors/divisors, 9×) vs عامل/عوامل (algebraic factors, 2×/3×) — the bank's principled split, matching the guide's divisor/factorisation distinction; خط (14×) vs لکیر (5×); کتنے ڈگری (3×, bank-consistent masculine usage, کتنی ڈگری 0×); بریکٹوں (2×, MAT-199/200) vs قوسین (10×); وتر double duty (hypotenuse + chord, 38×, established usage); قطعہ خط (MAT-265) vs قطعہ مستقیم (MAT-284).
- **Set family KEPT as transliterations (coordinator decision, guide-conflict flagged in §8):** سیٹ (60×), یونین, انٹرسیکشن, ذیلی سیٹ, خالی سیٹ are NOT converted to مجموعہ — مجموعہ occurs 55× meaning *sum* only, never set, and تقاطع already means cross-multiplication (MAT-130/132), so it would collide with intersection. - **Percentages:** prose `%`→فیصد (فیصد 59×); `%` survives ONLY in calculation lines, bare-number options, and value references (MAT-082/083 "65%"/"45%" as the tested value) — 79 of 122 EN `%` signs kept, 43 prose `%`→فیصد, zero `%` added (suite-pinned). Labeled formula lines keep `%` (MAT-144/146/147 `نفع % = 200 / 800 x 100 = 25%` — a formula, not prose; flagged in §8).
- **Echo-gloss rule applied:** مطابق نمونہ ("matching pattern") dropped in MAT-241/242/243 explanations (the preceding sentence already states the matching parts); MAT-257's two-sentence explanation kept (mirrors EN, not an echo).
- **Placeholders:** the ؟ series placeholder (MAT-476..485) is a symbol standing for a number — `31، ؟` spacing is correct typography and exempt from the no-space-before-؟ rule (validator + suite carry the exception; M5's attached `،؟` forms were reverted by normalization, §6). - **Coordinator decisions at merge (§6):** ڈھلان is the single slope term (ڈھلوان normalized away, 6×, MAT-355/356/358); coordinate compounds hyphenated throughout (x-محور 7×, y-محور 10×, x-مختصہ 10×, y-مختصہ 1×, x-مقطع 5×, y-مقطع 5×); MAT-082 اعشاری عدد for EN "as a decimal" (baseline اعشاریہ meant decimal *point* — wrong; flagged); MAT-104 pupils→شاگرد (bank pattern: pupils→شاگرد, students→طلبہ/طالب علم; MAT-104 was the sole outlier).
