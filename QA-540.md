## Failures found and fixed

The first QA run was **46/47 (exit 1)** — one failure, fixed before the final run:

1. **Near-duplicate stems (17 groups).** Nine groups were genuine collisions
   between new items and pre-existing banks (same fact, same wording):
   GKN-002/PAK-007, GKN-005/PAK-044, GKN-021/ISL-001, GKN-027/ISL-084,
   SS-002/PAK-008, SS-003/PAK-009, SS-004/PAK-010, SS-007/PAK-034,
   SS-009/PAK-036. Fixed by rewording only the nine **new** items' question
   stems (same tested fact, same options/answers/explanations); the
   pre-existing PAK/ISL items were not touched. The other eight groups were
   false positives from the first version of the stem normaliser, which stripped
   all non-Latin characters: Urdu-script questions collapsed to an empty stem
   (PPT-011/012/013, PPR-011/012/013) and Urdu-bank template stems
   ("What is the synonym/antonym of …", etc.) lost the Urdu word that makes
   them distinct. Fixed by making the normaliser Unicode-aware
   (`/[^\p{L}\p{N}\s]/gu`). After both fixes the check passes with zero groups.
   Residual note: the same fact is still tested in two banks in those nine
   cases (e.g. Surah count in both GK and Islamiyat) — that is cross-bank
   overlap by syllabus design, not a duplicate stem; whether to keep both is
   an owner/content decision, not a QA failure.

No other failures. `npx tsc --noEmit` also runs clean (exit 0) after these changes.
