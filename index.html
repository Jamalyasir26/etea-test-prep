Library — Notes

Short, exam-focused, original notes. Recall first: cover the note, recall it from memory, then read. Plain re-reading is low-utility (Dunlosky et al. 2013); retrieval practice is high-utility.

Practice — Topic Quizzes

20 questions per attempt, shuffled every time. Instant feedback after each question: correct answer + short explanation. All questions are original practice questions, labelled “AI-generated — not verified” (drafted by AI, structurally checked). They are not ETEA questions.

Each mock states which pattern variant it follows. ZamungKor mocks are disjoint (no question repeats across them). Track mocks draw from the shared practice bank — questions may repeat across track mocks; this is stated on each mock because the 520-question bank cannot supply 8+ disjoint 100-question mocks. All mocks are practice mocks, not official ETEA papers.

Flashcards — Spaced Repetition (SM-2-style)

Answer before flipping the card (retrieval practice). Grade yourself: Again / Hard / Good / Easy. Cards come back after 1 / 3 / 7 / 14 days — a scheduling aid, not a prescription (Dunlosky et al. 2013 rates distributed practice high-utility; the 1-3-7-14 schedule is a common practical starting point, not a prescription from Dunlosky).
